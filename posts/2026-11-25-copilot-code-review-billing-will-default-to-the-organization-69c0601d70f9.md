If you haven’t chosen how to bill Copilot code review for members with a Copilot license, those reviews will be billed to your organization starting December 30, 2026. To keep billing unchanged, select **Member** before then. This change applies to organizations and enterprises with Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise.

<a id="archive-heading-1"></a>

## [What’s changing](#archive-heading-1)

Today, when no billing choice is saved, reviews by members who have a paid Copilot license use the member’s Copilot entitlement. Starting December 30, 2026, those reviews will be billed to the organization that owns the repository. Accounts that don’t have AI Credits paid usage enabled, have a billing lock, or have no billable customer will be set to **Member** instead.

-   If you already chose **Member** or **Organization**, your choice stays in effect.
-   The separate setting for members without a Copilot license doesn’t change.

<a id="archive-heading-2"></a>

## [Keep member billing](#archive-heading-2)

Until then, unset settings show **Default (Member)** with a notice about the change. To keep using members’ Copilot entitlements, select **Member** in your enterprise or organization Copilot policy settings. That choice stays in effect after the default changes.

If your enterprise manages this setting, an enterprise owner makes this choice.

Organization billing requires AI Credits paid usage and can increase your organization’s spending.

To learn more, see [Configuring code review by GitHub Copilot](https://docs.github.com/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/set-up-copilot/configure-code-review).
