You can now share the GitHub Copilot plugins your team relies on without asking every member to repeat the same setup. Team customizations are generally available for organization teams.

Team maintainers and organization owners can select approved Agent Plugins as team defaults. This gives your team a shared starting point between individual customization and organization-wide policy.

<a id="archive-heading-1"></a>

### [Set up plugins for a team](#archive-heading-1)

If you’re a team maintainer or organization owner, open your organization team on github.com and select the **Customize Copilot** tab. From there, you can:

-   **Browse plugins** from the marketplaces built into Copilot, your enterprise’s marketplaces, and any marketplaces the team adds. You can then add individual plugins to the team.
-   **Add a marketplace** from a GitHub repository that contains a plugin marketplace. This makes its plugins available to browse. You can add individual plugins to the team to enable them.

Selected plugins are enabled by default for the team’s direct members in VS Code, the GitHub Copilot app, and Copilot CLI. Members of child teams don’t inherit them. Team defaults are optional. Members can turn a team plugin off in their Copilot client where policy permits.

<a id="archive-heading-2"></a>

### [Your policies still apply](#archive-heading-2)

Enterprise and organization policies take precedence over team choices. Copilot clients continue to enforce which marketplaces are allowed and which plugins are required or blocked. Team defaults don’t override those controls.

Team customizations are available for Copilot Business and Copilot Enterprise.

Learn more about [plugins for GitHub Copilot](https://docs.github.com/copilot/concepts/customizations/plugins) and join the discussion in [GitHub Community](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/211131).
