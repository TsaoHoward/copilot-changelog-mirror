Enterprise GitHub Apps can now call the [enterprise-level Copilot seat management APIs](https://docs.github.com/enterprise-cloud@latest/rest/copilot/copilot-user-management?apiVersion=2026-03-10) to control which users and enterprise teams are assigned Copilot seats.

The Copilot seat management APIs are accessible with a new enterprise fine-grained permission for Apps: `Enterprise Copilot license management`.
